## Why

The homepage is a feature sheet: it leads with counts and process vocabulary a visitor
cannot decode, and never says what the product does for the reader. A page that
cannot answer "what is this for me?" in one glance fails its job: getting installed.

## What

Rebuild `index.html` as a conversion landing: hero promises "your AI developer, with
receipts"; the reader's three failures (memory, smoke, mood) named first; a
verifiable proof (the repo maintains itself); the loop as four plain scenes; four
outcomes in plain language; an honest FAQ; one install CTA, twice. Keeps the hum
theme and the one-file static structure; moves nothing to README/docs that belongs
there already.
